Vipassana meditation

Sep 2021 · 10-day silent retreat
Dhamma Nilaya, Jaipur

Vipassana is a 2,500-year-old meditation technique, rediscovered by the Buddha, that teaches you to observe pain and pleasure as impermanent sensations without reacting to them. I completed a 10-day silent retreat at Dhamma Nilaya in Jaipur, rising at 4 AM and meditating until 9 PM with only brief breaks, and no devices or talking.

The experience was intense, both physically and mentally, but it changed how I work. By day 2 I could focus on my breath for over an hour, and getting through the sleepless nights around day 6 taught me resilience. Feel free to ask me anything about the practice.
